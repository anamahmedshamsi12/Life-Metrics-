Life Metrics Simulator

Customize your daily decisions and metrics

Define the metrics that matter to you, then describe three types of decisions you make in your day (Decision 1, Decision 2, Decision 3). The simulator will use these to track how your choices shape your week.

  • Examples of metrics: Focus, Social Energy, Health, Mood, Progress
  • Examples of decisions: “Stayed in to study”, “Balanced social + work”, “Procrastinated all day”
How the decisions-based mode works

You are setting up a simple model of your real daily choices:

  • Metrics = what you care about (e.g., grades, energy, mental health).
  • Decision 1 / 2 / 3 = common types of choices you make in a day (like “went to the gym” vs “scrolled TikTok for 3 hours”).
  • During the simulation, you just pick which decision your day felt closest to, and optionally log a note like “Went to mall instead of studying.”

You do not need to overthink it. Just capture a few patterns that show up in your real life and the tool will handle the tracking.
